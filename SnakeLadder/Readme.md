1. Board has cells from 1 to 100 (1D board)
2. Players will roll the 1 - 6 dice to decide moving steps
3. The player who reaches 100 will win
4. If player lands on Ladder start, they move up to the Ladder end
5. If player lands on Snake head, they move down to the snake tail
6. If a dice roll takes player beyond 100, they stay where they are
7. Ladder position and Snake postion is predefined
